01 / Source video

MP4 with H.264 is recommended for browser playback. Download your Drive video first. Start with a 30-second clip.

02 / Analyze a clip

YOLO weights download on first use. HOG needs no weights but often misses distant footballers. People are detected; names are confirmed by you.

03 / Camera & replay review

Camera cuts and repeated images are heuristic flags. Review every cut and mark replay ranges. A different replay angle may not be detected automatically.

Forecasts are suppressed on marked replays, suspected duplicate frames, missing identities, and expired confirmations.

04 / Export

How forecasting works

The bundled pilot uses a learned linear classifier on 26 causal motion and tentative ball-proximity features. Appearance and combined variants were compared using Chelsea-only episode cross-validation. The development holdout is Inter. Only 29 context-reviewed observations exist; a new third match and more labels are needed for independent validation. Ball blobs are heuristic, not reliable ball tracking.
