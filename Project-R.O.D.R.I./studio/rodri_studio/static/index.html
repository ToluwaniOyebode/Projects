01 / Source video

MP4 with H.264 is recommended for browser playback. Download your Drive video first. Start with a 30-second clip.

02 / Analyze a clip

YOLO weights download on first use. HOG needs no weights but often misses distant footballers. People are detected; names are confirmed by you. The Kaggle candidate requires its separate model package and is not the default.

03 / Camera & replay review

Camera cuts and repeated images are heuristic flags. Review every cut and mark replay ranges. A different replay angle may not be detected automatically.

Forecasts are suppressed on marked replays, suspected duplicate frames, missing identities, and expired confirmations.

04 / Export

Export detections, shot boundaries, identity confirmations, replay intervals and verified action labels as JSON.

How forecasting works

V4 compares up to three seconds of actor, ball and nearby-player context with shorter motion features. Missing history is masked after cuts or uncertain tracking. Reserved episodes stay out of training and model selection; unseen-match accuracy is unknown. Off-ball includes supporting and defensive movement. Conditional options assume an on-ball event occurs and do not measure unobserved intentions.
