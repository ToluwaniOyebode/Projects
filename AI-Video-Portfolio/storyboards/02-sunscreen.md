# 02 — Everyday Shield

**Format:** 9:16 · 15 seconds · Product-only sun-care ad

**Brief interpretation:** Make sunscreen feel like an easy, positive part of getting ready. Avoid implying that a product guarantees complete protection.

**Visual system:** Clean white and pale sand, sunlight through glass, fresh citrus accents, crisp highlights. Fictional unbranded sunscreen tube.

| Time | Shot | Motion / edit | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Bright beam of sunlight crosses a clean surface | Quick light sweep | Out into the day. |
| 2–5s | Sunscreen tube enters frame | Smooth vertical reveal | — |
| 5–8s | Abstract cream ribbon beside tube (not on skin) | Macro glide | Make room for daily care. |
| 8–12s | Product hero among sunlit glass shapes | Gentle orbit | A step in your routine. |
| 12–15s | Pack shot and CTA | Clean hold | Make it part of your day. |

**Sound:** Bright minimal percussion, airy whoosh, soft glass accents.

**Generation prompt:** “High-end sun care product advertisement, single minimalist white sunscreen tube with blank unbranded label, pale sand studio, sunlit glass refractions, abstract white cream ribbon on a clean acrylic surface, bright summer daylight, crisp premium cosmetic photography, smooth lateral camera move, no people, no hands, no faces, no skin, no text, vertical 9:16.”

**Negative prompt:** human, person, face, hands, skin, beach crowd, text, logos, medical claims, SPF number, watermark.

**Quality gate:** Do not invent SPF rating or protection claims. Use product-specific claims only when supplied and approved.
