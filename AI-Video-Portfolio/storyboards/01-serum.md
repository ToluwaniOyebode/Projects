**Format:** 9:16 · 15 seconds · Product-only visuals · Premium skincare spec ad

**Brief interpretation:** Make an everyday serum feel like a considered luxury ritual. One product, one message: a small daily moment worth slowing down for.

**Visual system:** Warm ivory, amber glass, soft directional sunlight, reflective stone, macro liquid texture, precise camera movement. Fictional unbranded bottle; replace with supplied product photography when authorized.

**Generation prompt:** “Premium editorial product commercial, single elegant amber glass skincare serum dropper bottle with completely blank unbranded label, warm ivory travertine plinth, soft directional morning sunlight, realistic glass reflections, macro cosmetic product photography, refined luxury campaign, shallow depth of field, slow controlled camera push, no people, no hands, no faces, no skin, no text, vertical 9:16.”
