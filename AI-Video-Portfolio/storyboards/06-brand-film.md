# 06 — The Daily Ritual

**Format:** 9:16 · 20 seconds · Multi-product skincare campaign

**Brief interpretation:** Connect a small collection into one coherent routine. Use a fictional unbranded product family until authorized brand assets are supplied.

**Visual system:** A consistent warm-neutral studio, matching blank labels, soft daylight, clean geometric plinths and a recurring amber accent.

| Time | Shot | Motion / edit | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Three products arranged in silhouette | Slow reveal | A ritual, in every step. |
| 3–6s | Serum hero close-up | Macro push | Step 01 · Serum |
| 6–9s | Cleanser hero close-up | Lateral slide | Step 02 · Cleanse |
| 9–12s | Moisturizer hero close-up | Gentle orbit | Step 03 · Moisturize |
| 12–16s | Trio composition with matching light | Pull-back; rhythmic cuts | Made to work together. |
| 16–20s | Final collection lockup | Hold for CTA | Build your daily ritual. |

**Sound:** One consistent ambient track with three subtle percussive accents for the product steps.

**Generation prompt:** “Premium cohesive skincare collection commercial, exactly three coordinated unbranded products: one amber serum dropper bottle, one white cleanser tube, one ivory moisturizer jar, matching blank labels, warm ivory studio, pale stone plinths, soft natural daylight, amber accent reflections, high-end editorial product photography, slow elegant camera movement, no people, no hands, no faces, no skin, no text, vertical 9:16.”

**Negative prompt:** people, face, hands, skin, text, logo, inconsistent packaging, duplicate products, watermark, medical claims.

**Quality gate:** Use the same reference image or seed for packaging continuity. Keep product names and claims generic unless supplied by a real client.
