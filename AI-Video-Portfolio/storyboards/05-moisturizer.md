# 05 — Soft Focus

**Format:** 9:16 · 15 seconds · Moisturizer product ad

**Brief interpretation:** Make the product feel comforting and premium with texture, light and a restrained pace.

**Visual system:** Soft cream, pale taupe, natural stone, botanical shadow, gentle daylight. Fictional unbranded cream jar.

| Time | Shot | Motion / edit | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Soft botanical shadow moves across cream backdrop | Slow drift | Make space for softness. |
| 3–6s | Moisturizer jar rotates into light | Gentle 20-degree orbit | — |
| 6–9s | Macro cream swirl on a neutral ceramic surface | Slow macro slide | A softer kind of ritual. |
| 9–13s | Jar and lid arranged as a hero still life | Slow pull-back | Care, considered. |
| 13–15s | Product lockup | Hold | Discover your daily care. |

**Sound:** Soft piano texture, warm ambient bed, delicate ceramic tap.

**Generation prompt:** “Quiet luxury moisturizer campaign, single elegant cream jar with blank unbranded label and matching lid, warm ivory ceramic, pale taupe stone, soft botanical shadows, diffused window light, tactile cream swirl on separate ceramic palette, refined editorial cosmetic still life, slow controlled camera slide, no people, no hands, no faces, no skin, no text, vertical 9:16.”

**Negative prompt:** people, face, hands, skin, typography, logos, extra jars, medical claims, watermark.

**Quality gate:** Avoid before/after skin claims or generated skin textures. Keep jar and lid proportions consistent.
