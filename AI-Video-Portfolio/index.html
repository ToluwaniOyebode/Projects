02 / How I work

From loose brief to finished creative.

Creative direction

  1. Identify the audience, product truth and single message.
  2. Define the hook, visual language and CTA.
  3. Build a shot-by-shot storyboard before generating.
  4. Use approved product references and consistent visual prompts.

Production & finishing

  1. Generate product-only visuals and motion; never synthetic faces or skin.
  2. Select the strongest takes and maintain continuity.
  3. Edit pacing, typography, transitions, music and sound design.
  4. Review brand fit, claims, legibility and 9:16 export quality.

Planned toolchain: Higgsfield for AI product visuals and motion; Adobe / Premiere or After Effects for editing and finishing. Tools will be listed as used only after the work is actually produced.
